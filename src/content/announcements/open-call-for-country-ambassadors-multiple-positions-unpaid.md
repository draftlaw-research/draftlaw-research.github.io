## **📢 We’re hiring Country Ambassadors | Draft Law Research | Multiple Positions | Voluntary, Unpaid Position**

Are you an undergraduate law student who wants to build your academic profile while helping grow a global community of legal scholars?

Draft Law Research is looking for 2-3 Country Ambassadors per country to represent our organization and help it grow. They will be responsible for promoting our programs, specifically in the country they are choosing to represent. 

### **What you’ll do**

- Promote Draft Law Research, our Mentor-Student Co-Author Program, and our blog in your university, network, and country
- Recruit and coordinate fellow students for the Mentor-Student Co-Author Program
- Share and promote new blog posts, calls for papers, events, and announcements on social media and in academic groups
- Encourage students and peers to read, engage with, and contribute to the blog
- Serve as the first point of contact for students and institutions in your country
- Connect us with law schools, student societies, journals, and potential mentors
- Collect feedback from participants and report to the core team
- Help organize webinars, info sessions, and outreach campaigns
- Submit a short monthly activity report

### **What you’ll gain**

- ✅ Certificate of Involvement
- ✅ Personalized Letter of Recommendation (on successful completion of the term)
- ✅ Networking with mentors, researchers, and ambassadors worldwide
- ✅ Leadership and academic-outreach experience for your CV
- ✅ Priority consideration for our programs and opportunities

### **Details**

- 🕒 Term: 6 months, with the option to renew
- 🤝 This is a voluntary, unpaid position
- 💻 Fully remote; about 3-5 hours per week
- 🌍 Open to applicants from any country

### **Eligibility**

- You must be an **active undergraduate law student** (LL.B or equivalent)
- You must **remain enrolled as a student for the entire six-month term**, so your expected graduation date must fall after the term ends
- Renewal is possible only if you are still an active undergraduate student at that time
- Strong communication skills, interest in legal research, and the initiative to lead in your community
