  contemporary law. However, its legal significance remains inherently dependent
  upon processes of communication, interpretation, and meaning-making. This
  Special Issue approaches sustainability not as a fixed legal category but as a
  semiotic construct whose meaning is continuously produced, negotiated,
  translated, and contested through legal language, discourse, institutional
  practices, and interpretative communities.
