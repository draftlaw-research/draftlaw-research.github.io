Elahi is an MSc candidate in Sociology of Law at Lund University, currently a part-time Research Consultant with the Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung Rule of Law Programme Asia and an intern at Lund's Division of Risk Management and Societal Safety (LTH). He previously spent two years researching Rohingya refugee governance and access to justice at BRAC University's Centre for Peace and Justice; his interests center on refugee law, forced displacement, and statelessness.
