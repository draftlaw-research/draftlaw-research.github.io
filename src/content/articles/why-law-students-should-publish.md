Most law students first meet peer review when they receive their first rejection. That is a late and expensive way to learn what a reviewer looks for.

## What review teaches

A reviewer asks the questions a tired reader will ask: what is the claim, what supports it, and what has been left out. Answering those questions once, on a small piece, changes how you write everything afterwards.

## A low-risk way to start

Start with a case summary or a short commentary. Keep the claim small and the sources careful. The goal is to practise the whole process, from idea to published page.
