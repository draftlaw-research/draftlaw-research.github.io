**Ishaq Harisu** is an LL.B. student at the Faculty of Law, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, Nigeria, having previously completed a Diploma in Law. He currently serves as the Country Representative of Juris Publica Nigeria under Juris Publica International. He is also involved in student leadership and legal advocacy, having served in various leadership and media-related positions within the university's legal community. His interests include Islamic law, human rights law, constitutional law, public interest litigation, legal research and advocacy. He is passionate about promoting legal awareness, strengthening access to justice, and creating opportunities for collaboration among law students. Through his academic engagements, leadership experience and advocacy initiatives, he hopes to contribute meaningfully to legal development and the advancement of justice in society.
