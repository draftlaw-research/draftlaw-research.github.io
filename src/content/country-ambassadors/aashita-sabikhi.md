**Aashita Sabikhi** is a fifth-year law student at The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, India, and currently serves as a Country Ambassador for DraftLaw Research. She has a keen interest in exploring the relationship between legal principles and their practical application, particularly how law shapes society, safeguards rights, and facilitates meaningful change. Through internships, moot court competitions, debates, and other academic engagements, she has actively sought to broaden her legal understanding beyond the classroom. She enjoys exploring unconventional legal questions, challenging established perspectives, and approaching complex issues from different angles. Beyond her academic pursuits, Aashita has a lively sense of humour and firmly believes that coffee deserves recognition as a fundamental right, particularly before morning court sessions!
