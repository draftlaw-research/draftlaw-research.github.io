**Raji Usman Amao** is a penultimate-year law student at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, Nigeria, with a keen interest in legal writing, research, and public speaking. His academic interests span Energy Law, Taxation and Finance, Litigation, Artificial Intelligence, Climate Change, and Dispute Resolution. He has published articles on various platforms, including Opinion Nigeria, BarristerNG, [Sabilaw.org](http://Sabilaw.org), and African Angle. An active participant in debate and moot court competitions, he has earned national and international awards and recognition for his contributions and achievements. His commitment to understanding procedural law and developing his advocacy skills has also led him to engage with various professional organizations and associations, where he seeks to contribute meaningfully to the legal community and society. He currently serves as the Vice President of the Tax Club at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, where he coordinates the Club's activities across the Kongo and Samaru campuses and promotes awareness of taxation, tax audits, and annual tax return filing. Through this role, he hopes to contribute to the development of tax law and financial awareness while advancing his broader aspiration to create meaningful change in the legal and financial landscape.
