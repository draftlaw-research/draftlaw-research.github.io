**Syeda Fahama Ali Zaidi** is a fourth-year law student at Government College University Lahore (GCUL), with a keen interest in legal research, academic development, and advocacy. She currently serves as the Head of Moot Guild at the Ravian Law Society, where she contributes to promoting mooting, legal research, and advocacy skills among students. She has also been actively involved in various legal and youth organizations, holding roles as a Human Rights Liaison, Department and Campus Ambassador, and member of discipline and management teams. These experiences have helped her develop strong leadership, teamwork, communication, and organizational skills. Her academic interests include Constitutional Law, Human Rights Law, Criminal Law, and International Law, with a particular focus on contemporary legal issues, legal research, and the development of analytical and advocacy skills. She aspires to deepen her legal knowledge and contribute meaningfully to the legal profession.
