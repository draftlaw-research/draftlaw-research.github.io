**Satveer Singh** is a first-year LLB student at the University of Birmingham and the United Kingdom Country Ambassador for DraftLaw Research. His academic interests include public law, legal ethics, and access to justice, alongside a growing interest in legal research and academic writing.

He participated in the Sikhs in Law Advocacy Training Weekend in Birmingham, where he engaged with civil and criminal case materials and developed skills in case analysis, legal reasoning, and oral advocacy. He has also volunteered with MICBA Forum Italia and Canadian Food for Children in Canada, strengthening his communication, teamwork, and community engagement.

As a Country Ambassador, Satveer aims to connect law students across the UK with opportunities in research, mentorship, and academic publishing. He believes that exploring opportunities beyond the classroom can help students develop practical skills, broaden their perspectives, and build meaningful professional connections.
