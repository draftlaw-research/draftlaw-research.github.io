**Maxine Wanjiru Mbugua** is a Kenyan law student at the Catholic University of Eastern Africa, with a strong interest in legal research, human rights, access to justice and child protection. She is the Co-Founder of Kitara Foundation, a student-led initiative committed to improving the lives of children through community-centred interventions. She was selected for the Aspire Leaders Program Cohort 4 (2026) and participated in the inaugural African Unbound Democratic Debate, representing Kenya. Through her academic, leadership and community engagement experiences, she is passionate about advancing inclusive opportunities, strengthening legal scholarship and contributing to meaningful social impact. She aspires to pursue a career that bridges legal research, advocacy and institutional development, particularly in advancing justice and protecting vulnerable communities.
