**Madina Zaur Khalilli** is a Bachelor of Laws student at ADA University in Baku, Azerbaijan, currently serving as a Country Ambassador for DraftLaw Research and a surveyor for the State Statistics Committee. Her legal experience includes internships with Justice Volunteers, focusing on criminal risk and probation evaluations, and the Record of Law Research Program. Beyond legal practice, she co-founded and manages events for the disability inclusion initiative Məndən yox, Mənimlə danış, co-founded the SUITS High School Legal Club, and contributes to ADAlılar Magazine. She has also volunteered at major international forums, including COP29 and the World Urban Forum (WUF13). Passionate about legal advocacy, human rights, gender equity, and disability accessibility, she aspires to advance inclusive legal frameworks and public policy through comparative legal research and international diplomacy.
