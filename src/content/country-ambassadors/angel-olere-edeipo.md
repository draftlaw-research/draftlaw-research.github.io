**Angel Olere Edeipo** is a 400-level Law student at the University of Benin, Nigeria, with a keen interest in legal research, legal writing, arbitration, and criminal law. She is passionate about advancing legal knowledge, promoting access to legal scholarship and exploring the practical application of law in addressing societal challenges.

She serves as a 400-Level Class Representative on the Law Students Bar Association (LAWBA) Board and is a student member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (CIArb). She has also participated in legal development programmes and contributed to legal writing, including an article examining the effectiveness of arbitration in achieving justice in Nigeria. Her extracurricular engagements have strengthened her skills in leadership, communication, research and organization.

She aspires to contribute meaningfully to legal scholarship, academic collaboration and the development of the legal profession.
