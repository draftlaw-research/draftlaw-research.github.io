**Youssef Badrawy Roshdy** is a fourth-year law student at the Faculty of Law, Capital University, Egypt. His academic interests include artificial intelligence and the legal profession, technology law, intellectual property law, corporate law, and international commercial arbitration. He has served as Head of the Human Resources Committee at the Youth Entity of Future Builders | Ministry of Youth & Sports of Egypt in Giza Governorate and as a Supervisor of the Platform Support Department at Vital. His interests extend to legal research, writing, and emerging legal challenges.
