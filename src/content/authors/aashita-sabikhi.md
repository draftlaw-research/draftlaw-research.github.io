**Aashita Sabikhi** is a fifth-year B.A. LL.B. (Hons.) student at the Faculty of Law, The Maharaja Sayajirao University of Baroda, with a diploma in Legal Journalism. Her achievements include winning a Model United Nations competition at MSU Baroda, being named Best Mooter at the IIMT Noida Moot Court Competition, and securing runner-up position in a trial advocacy competition organised by Gujarat University. She has also gained legal internship experience with public sector undertakings, including GAIL India and GETCO. Aashita believes that deadlines have a way of inspiring miracles. She loves the courtroom, dislikes early mornings, and firmly believes that coffee deserves recognition as a fundamental right.
