**Apoorva Sharma** is a final-year LL.B. student at the Department of Laws, Panjab University, Chandigarh. Her interests lie in legal research, writing, and courtroom practice, particularly in exploring how judicial decisions shape constitutional interpretation, individual autonomy, and fundamental rights in a democratic society. She views law as a dynamic institution shaped by constitutional values and social realities. Aspiring to contribute meaningfully to the legal profession, Apoorva seeks opportunities for academic and professional growth through internships, research projects, and collaborative legal work, with a commitment to advancing positive social change through law.
