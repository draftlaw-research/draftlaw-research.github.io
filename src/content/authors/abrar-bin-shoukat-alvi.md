**Abrar-Bin-Shoukat Alvi** is a final year LL.B. Candidate at the Department of Law, Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP). His scholarship bridges public international law, environmental law, green criminology, cyber law, digital rights, and data protection. His research interrogates Rohingya displacement, legal liminality, and digital-humanitarian governance, themes explored in his forthcoming article on biometric registration, humanitarian dependency, and consent in the inaugural LEB Journal of Law and Policy. He has presented at Daffodil International University’s 1st Decolonizing Law and Criminology Conference, the NSU Undergraduate Law Symposium, and the IUB Student Research Conference. An accomplished Jessup, Stetson, and Henry Dunant moot oralist, he was named Bangladesh’s Second Best Oralist at Stetson and represented Bangladesh at the 2026 Jessup International Rounds in Washington, D.C.
