**Lívia Archanjo Deretti** is a Brazilian undergraduate student pursuing dual degrees in Law at the Pontifícia Universidade Católica do Paraná (PUCPR) and International Relations at the Centro Universitário Internacional (UNINTER). A member of the Center for Studies in International Law and International Resolutions (NEDRI) at PUCPR, she has experience in Brazilian and international case law research, academic writing in Portuguese and English, and comparative analysis of judicial decisions. Fluent in English and based in Curitiba, she is passionate about international law and intends to build her career in the field.
