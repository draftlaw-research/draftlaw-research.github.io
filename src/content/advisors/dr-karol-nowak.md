**Dr. Karol Nowak** is a Doctor of Laws and Associate Professor of Law at Lund University, Sweden, where he is the former Director of the Master's Programme in International Human Rights Law. His research and teaching focus on human rights law, constitutional law and procedural law. He is particularly interested in protecting individuals from state abuse, the right to a fair trial and the presumption of innocence. He also studies counter-terrorism and serious crime policy in Europe, including profiling, interrogation and evidence gathering, and how human rights law relates to democracy.

He has worked with the European Commission. He was previously a researcher at the Raoul Wallenberg Institute of Human Rights and Humanitarian Law (RWI), where he is now an Affiliated Scholar, and a lecturer at the University of Gothenburg. He has taken part in EU-funded research projects, including DETECTER and SURVEILLE, and works with RWI as a liaison officer, lecturing and training police, judges, lawyers and prosecutors in a number of countries.

Dr. Nowak is Lund University's coordinator for the European Master's Programme in Human Rights and Democratisation ([E.MA](http://E.MA)) in Venice. He is also an affiliated researcher at Lund's Centre for Preparedness and Resilience (LUPREP), and a principal investigator at the Lund Human Rights Research Hub. He directs courses on procedural law of human rights and human rights in the field, and the Nordic moot court competition.
