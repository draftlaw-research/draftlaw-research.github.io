# **DraftLaw Research | International Opportunities**

DraftLaw Research invites applications from motivated law students, graduates, legal researchers, academics and professionals to join its Editorial Team in various capacities.

DraftLaw Research is an international legal research and academic publishing initiative committed to making legal scholarship accessible, encouraging critical legal thinking and mentoring the next generation of legal scholars.

As our blog continues to welcome contributions from writers across different jurisdictions and areas of law, we are expanding our editorial team to strengthen the quality, consistency and academic standards of our publications.

We are looking for individuals with strong legal research, analytical, writing and editorial skills who are interested in contributing to an international legal publication.

* * *

# 1\. Senior Editor

**Responsibilities**

Senior Editors will oversee the editorial process and ensure that submissions meet DraftLaw Research's academic and publication standards.

Their responsibilities will include:

-   Conducting the initial editorial assessment of newly received submissions.
    
-   Determining whether a submission should proceed to blind review or be rejected at the preliminary stage.
    
-   Assessing the relevance, originality, academic quality, structure, and general suitability of submissions.
    
-   Reviewing the recommendations and assessment reports submitted by Associate Editors.
    
-   Making editorial decisions on whether a manuscript should be rejected, returned for revision, or proceed towards publication.
    
-   Communicating editorial decisions, revision requests, and other relevant matters to authors.
    
-   Reviewing copy-edited manuscripts and overseeing the final stages of publication.
    
-   Ensuring consistency in editorial standards and procedures.
    
-   Providing guidance to Associate Editors and other members of the editorial team.
    

Senior Editors may also undertake academic reviews of submissions where they have relevant subject expertise.

**Qualifications**

Applicants should preferably have:

-   An LL.B. or equivalent law degree; an LL.M. or higher qualification will be an advantage.
    
-   Strong legal research, analytical, and academic writing skills.
    
-   Experience in academic publishing, editorial work, peer review, or legal research.
    
-   A good understanding of legal scholarship and academic publication standards.
    
-   The ability to assess legal arguments, research methodology, and the use of legal authorities critically.
    

Prior editorial or academic publishing experience will be an advantage.

* * *

# 2\. Associate Editor

**Responsibilities**

Associate Editors will be responsible for the substantive academic assessment of assigned manuscripts. They will also act as the primary reviewer of the manuscripts assigned to them.

Their responsibilities will include:

-   Receiving anonymized submissions assigned by Senior Editors.
    
-   Conducting a detailed assessment of the manuscript's originality, relevance, academic quality, structure, coherence, and analytical depth.
    
-   Examining the strength and accuracy of legal arguments, research methodology, reasoning, and supporting authorities.
    
-   Identifying factual inaccuracies, gaps in analysis, unsupported conclusions, and weaknesses in the use of legal sources.
    
-   Assessing whether the manuscript makes a meaningful contribution to its subject area.
    
-   Preparing a structured assessment report with clear and constructive comments.
    
-   Recommending whether the manuscript should be rejected, returned to the author for revisions, or accepted for copy-editing and publication.
    
-   Providing specific reasons for any recommendation to reject or request revisions.
    
-   Identifying the changes required before a manuscript can be considered for publication.
    
-   Forwarding the assessment report and recommendation to the Senior Editor.
    
-   Where appropriate, suggesting that an additional specialist review be obtained for submissions requiring expertise beyond their own subject knowledge.
    

Associate Editors are expected to undertake both editorial assessment and academic review. They should therefore accept assignments only where they have sufficient knowledge of the relevant subject.

**Qualifications**

Applicants should preferably have:

-   An LL.B. or equivalent law degree.
    
-   Strong legal research, analytical, and academic writing skills.
    
-   Familiarity with legal research methods, primary and secondary legal sources, and academic citation practices.
    
-   The ability to critically assess legal arguments and academic manuscripts.
    
-   Previous research, publication, reviewing, or editorial experience will be an advantage.
    

* * *

# 3\. Copy Editor

**Responsibilities**

Copy Editors will be responsible for improving the language, clarity, consistency, and presentation of manuscripts that have been approved to proceed towards publication.

Their responsibilities will include:

-   Correcting grammatical, spelling, punctuation, and language errors.
    
-   Improving sentence structure, readability, and clarity without changing the author's intended meaning.
    
-   Ensuring consistency in terminology, abbreviations, headings, and formatting.
    
-   Identifying unclear, repetitive, or unnecessarily complicated passages.
    
-   Checking the consistency of quotations, footnotes, references, and other formatting elements.
    
-   Ensuring that manuscripts comply with DraftLaw Research's editorial and formatting guidelines.
    
-   Highlighting all changes made during the copy-editing process.
    
-   Preparing a clean and clearly marked-up version of the edited manuscript for review by the Senior Editor.
    
-   Raising queries where clarification or author input is required.
    

Copy Editors will focus on language and presentation rather than independently assessing the substantive legal merits of a manuscript. They must preserve the author's academic voice and arguments.

**Qualifications**

Applicants should preferably have:

-   Excellent written English and a strong command of grammar and punctuation.
    
-   Demonstrable proofreading, editing, or copy-editing skills.
    
-   Applicants with backgrounds in English, linguistics, journalism, publishing, law or related fields may apply.
    
-   Having relevant editing experience will be an advantage.

# A. General Eligibility Requirements

Applicants should demonstrate:

-   A genuine interest in legal scholarship, academic publishing and research.
    
-   Strong written communication and analytical abilities.
    
-   Professionalism, reliability and the ability to meet agreed deadlines.
    
-   Willingness to work collaboratively with an international editorial team.
    
-   Respect for academic integrity, confidentiality and editorial independence.
    
-   Commitment to impartiality, constructive feedback and responsible scholarship.
    

Applicants from different jurisdictions and legal education systems are welcome.

Unless a particular position specifies otherwise, applications are open to undergraduate law students, postgraduate students, law graduates, legal researchers and legal professionals.

Previous editorial or publication experience is desirable but not mandatory for every position. We particularly encourage applications from individuals who demonstrate strong research abilities, academic potential and a willingness to develop their editorial skills.

# B. Working Arrangements

-   **Nature of positions:** Voluntary and unpaid.
    
-   **Location:** Fully remote.
    
-   **Recruitment:** International.
    
-   **Working arrangements:** Flexible, subject to assigned responsibilities and agreed deadlines.
    
-   **Collaboration:** Online coordination with the DraftLaw Research editorial team.
    
-   **Duration:** Six Months or One Year (subject to renewal)
    

All team members are expected to maintain professional standards, respect confidentiality and complete their assigned responsibilities within agreed timelines.

# C. What You Will Gain

Joining the DraftLaw Research Editorial Team offers an opportunity to:

-   Gain practical experience in academic publishing and editorial management.
    
-   Develop advanced legal research, analytical and writing skills.
    
-   Learn how academic manuscripts are assessed, reviewed and prepared for publication.
    
-   Work alongside an international network of law students, researchers and legal professionals.
    
-   Develop subject-specific expertise through engagement with diverse legal scholarship.
    
-   Contribute to the development of an accessible international legal research platform.
    
-   Build professional experience in academic editing, peer review and publication.
    

Certificates of Involvement and Letters of Recommendation may be considered for team members who demonstrate satisfactory commitment and performance during their tenure, subject to the organisation's assessment and applicable policies.
