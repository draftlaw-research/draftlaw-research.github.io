## About the program

Each cycle, experienced legal researchers bring an original research idea and work with 2 to 3 undergraduate co-authors over one semester. Together they take a project from conception to journal-ready submission, learning how to identify a research question, build an argument, write for publication, and navigate the academic process.

Students learn by doing. Mentors publish. Everyone grows.

## For mentors

### What we look for

- LL.M or higher, or equivalent professional experience
- At least one peer-reviewed or professionally published piece
- Commitment to ethical authorship and clear credit

### Responsibilities

- Bring an original research idea
- Guide 2 to 3 student co-authors through one semester
- Give about 3 to 4 hours per month
- Support the team until the paper is ready to submit to a journal

### Who can apply

Legal researchers, practitioners and academics from any jurisdiction.

### How to apply

Email us at draftlawresearch@gmail.com with a short note on your background, area of expertise and availability. Watch the [announcements](/programs/announcements/) for open calls.

## For students

### What we look for

- Currently enrolled in an LL.B or LL.M program
- Curiosity and a topic you care about
- Respect for citation and integrity standards

### Responsibilities

- Draft, revise and meet deadlines
- Work as part of a small co-author team
- Take part in meetings with your mentor through the semester

### Who can apply

Undergraduate law students from any jurisdiction.

### How to apply

Email us at draftlawresearch@gmail.com with a short note on your studies, areas of interest and why you would like to join. Watch the [announcements](/programs/announcements/) for open calls.
