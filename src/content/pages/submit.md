Submitting is completely free. No charges or fees apply. Each submission goes through blind peer review, so a submission does not guarantee publication.

## What we accept

Short Articles, Case Summaries, Legal Commentary, Short Book Reviews and Law Opinion Pieces.

## Topics

Any area of law that will cater to an international audience.

### Suggested themes

- Human rights and international law
- Constitutional and public law
- Criminal law and justice
- Technology, AI, and law
- Environmental and climate law
- Gender, access to justice, and legal reform
- Refugee law
- Migration law
- Humanitarian law
- Business law
- Comparative law and emerging legal systems, including South Asia
- Law and other disciplines (economics, medicine, sociology, biology, etc.)

## Submission guidelines

- All submissions should abide by the rules mentioned on this page.
- All submissions must be made in .doc or .docx format.
- Name the file in the following format: `Title_Content-Type_AuthorName.doc`
- Send your submission to [submission.draftlaw@gmail.com](mailto:submission.draftlaw@gmail.com).
- All submissions are accepted on a rolling basis.
- All submissions must be accompanied by a short author bio and a photo.

## Content guidelines

- **Short Article:** original argument or analysis, about 1,000 to 1,500 words.
- **Case Summary:** facts, issue, holding, and a brief note on significance, about 600 to 1,000 words.
- **Legal Commentary:** analysis of a law, amendment, judgment, or policy development, about 800 to 1,200 words.
- **Short Book Review:** review of a recent law or law-related book, about 600 to 1,000 words.
- **Law Opinion Piece:** a clear position on a legal issue, supported by argument, about 800 to 1,200 words.

## Special notes

- All submissions must be original and written by the author. Content must not exceed 15% plagiarism (as per standard plagiarism detection tools).
- If you want to use any charts, figures or relevant pictures, you may include them in the Word file.
- Submissions go through a mandatory AI detection check. Any submission made with generative AI text will be rejected.
- The writing should be professional, clear, and accessible. Avoid excessive legal jargon unless necessary, and where used, explain it simply.
- The word limit can be relaxed if a piece requires a larger discussion.

## Formatting guidelines

- **Font:** Georgia Pro
- **Title font size:** 16
- **Body font size:** 12
- **Line spacing:** 1.5
- **Alignment:** Justified
- **Citation:** We do not accept footnotes or endnotes. Please cite the sources you use through hyperlinks.

## Review process

- All submissions go through a preliminary editorial review.
- Selected submissions are then subjected to a blind peer review.
- You may be asked to make edits or revisions based on feedback.
- Once approved, your piece may be published on our website and social media platforms. Proper author credits will be given.
