**Tasnuba Tasnim Anita** is a law graduate from Independent University, Bangladesh, having completed her LL.B (Hons.), cum laude. Her interests lie in legal research, advocacy, international law, and human rights. She currently interns at the Attorney General’s Office under the UNDP Internship Programme. A former General Secretary of the IUB Moot Court Society, she has received recognition in national and international moot court competitions and actively contributes to legal education, and academic initiatives. Her research interests include public international law, international environmental and maritime law, human rights, and legal policy. Through her academic, research, and advocacy experiences, she aims to contribute to legal scholarship and policy development, while pursuing further studies and building a career in international law and public service.
