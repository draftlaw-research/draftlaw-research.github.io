**Sowrov Talukder** is an LL.B. (Hons) candidate at Green University of Bangladesh with a strong interest in public international law and human rights. He is actively involved in international moot court competitions, legal research, memorial drafting, and oral advocacy. He is a Millennium Fellow and currently serves as the Newsletter Editor at the Bangladesh Center for Refugee Law Studies. His research interests include environmental and climate law, refugee protection, and human rights. He aspires to contribute to legal research, policy development, and advocacy in the fields of climate justice and refugee protection.
