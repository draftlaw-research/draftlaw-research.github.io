**Razib Hossain Faisal** completed his Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) at the Department of Law, American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). His research interests span data protection and privacy, artificial intelligence governance, digital rights, constitutional and international law, human rights, and climate justice, with a particular focus on Bangladesh’s evolving legal landscape. He is interested in examining how legal frameworks can address emerging technological, social, and environmental challenges, particularly those concerning fundamental rights, accountability, and access to justice.

His academic interests lie at the intersection of law, public policy, and institutional responsibility. He aspires to pursue a career in legal research and practice, contributing to legal scholarship, evidence-based policymaking, and effective legal reform. Through his work, he aims to connect legal analysis with practical solutions that promote responsible technological governance, strengthen human rights protections, and advance justice for vulnerable communities.
