**Saimum Ara Vabna** is a student at Bangladesh University of Professionals (BUP), with research interests in human rights, criminal justice, drug addiction prevention, narcotics-related offences, tea garden workers’ rights, and constitutional law in Bangladesh. Her research focuses on human rights protection, access to justice, labor rights, and legal safeguards for vulnerable and marginalized communities. She aspires to contribute to legal research, human rights advocacy, and evidence-based policy development to promote justice, equality, and the rule of law in Bangladesh.
